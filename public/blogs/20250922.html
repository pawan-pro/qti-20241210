USD - Crude Oil Inventories

Date: 24-Sep-25, Wed, 20:00 IST Currency: USD Importance: 3 Previous: 3.939M Notes: The Energy Information Administration's (EIA) Crude Oil Inventories measures the weekly change in the number of barrels of commercial crude oil held by US firms. The level of inventories influences the price of petroleum products, which can have an impact on inflation. If the increase in crude inventories is more than expected, it implies weaker demand and is bearish for crude prices. The same can be said if a decline in inventories is less than expected. If the increase in crude is less than expected, it implies greater demand and is bullish for crude prices. The same can be said if a decline in inventories is more than expected. This report is closely watched by energy traders and can have significant impacts on oil prices and related energy sector equities. Learn More: USOILRoll

JPY - Japan - Autumn Equinox Day

Date: 24-Sep-25, Wed, All Day Currency: JPY Importance: 3 Notes: Autumn Equinox Day (Shūbun no Hi) is a national holiday in Japan that falls on September 23rd each year (or September 22nd in some years). It is one of the "Higan" periods, which are times for visiting ancestral graves and paying respects to deceased family members. The holiday often results in reduced business activity in Japan as many people take time off for family observances and travel. This can affect economic data releases and market liquidity during the week. Learn More: Legacy Reference

CAD - Canada - Autumn Equinox Day
