JPY - Japan - Autumn Equinox

Date: 23-Sep-25, Tue, All Day Currency: JPY Importance: 3 Notes: Autumn Equinox Day (Shūbun no Hi) is a public holiday in Japan that celebrates the autumn equinox. It's part of the "Higan" period, when Buddhist temples hold special services and many people visit graves of their ancestors. The holiday often results in reduced business activity in Japan as many people take time off. This can affect economic data releases and market liquidity during the week. USD - S&P Global Manufacturing PMI (Sep) P

Date: 23-Sep-25, Tue, 19:15 IST Currency: USD Importance: 3 Forecast: Link Previous: 53 Notes: The Manufacturing Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI) measures the activity level of purchasing managers in the manufacturing sector. A reading above 50 indicates expansion in the sector; below 50 indicates contraction. Traders watch these surveys closely as purchasing managers usually have early access to data about their company's performance, which can be a leading indicator of overall economic performance. A higher than expected reading should be taken as positive/bullish for the USD while a lower than expected reading should be taken as negative/bearish for the USD. Learn More: USDJPY EURUSD

USD - S&P Global Services PMI (Sep) P

Date: 23-Sep-25, Tue, 19:15 IST Currency: USD Importance: 3 Forecast: Link Previous: 54.5 Notes: The Service PMI release is published monthly by Markit Economics. The data are based on surveys of over 400 executives in private sector service companies. The surveys cover transport and communication, financial intermediaries, business and personal services, computing & IT, hotels and restaurants. An index level of 50 denotes no change since the previous month, while a level above 50 signals an improvement, and below 50 indicates a deterioration. A reading that is stronger than forecast is generally supportive (bullish) for the USD, while a weaker than forecast reading is generally negative (bearish) for the USD. Learn More: USDJPY EURUSD

ZAR - South Africa - Heritage Day

Date: 24-Sep-25, Wed, All Day Currency: ZAR Importance: 3 Notes: Heritage Day is a public holiday in South Africa that is celebrated on 24 September. It was established in 1995 to recognize and celebrate the cultural wealth of the nation's diverse population. On this day, South Africans are encouraged to take pride in their heritage, including languages, culture, and traditions. Government offices, banks, and many businesses are closed. The holiday can affect rand liquidity in financial markets and may impact trade flows between South Africa and its partners. Learn More: Legacy Reference

USD - GDP (QoQ) (Q2)

Date: 25-Sep-25, Thur, 18:00 IST Currency: USD Importance: 3 Forecast: 3.30% Previous: -0.50% Notes: Gross Domestic Product (GDP) measures the annualized change in the inflation-adjusted value of all goods and services produced by the economy. It is the broadest measure of economic activity and the primary indicator of the economy's health. Usual Effect: Actual > Forecast = Good for currency. Frequency: Released monthly. There are 3 versions of GDP released a month apart - Advance, second release and Final. Both the advance the second release are tagged as preliminary in the economic calendar. A reading that is stronger than forecast is generally supportive (bullish) for the USD, while a weaker than forecast reading is generally negative (bearish) for the USD. Learn More: USDJPY EURUSD
