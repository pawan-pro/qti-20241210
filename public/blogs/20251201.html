Key Economic Events: Week Of 1st December 2025
Release Date: 1st December 2025
What to Expect in Markets This Week: The week features key manufacturing data from China and the US (ISM PMI). Investors will closely watch Fed Chair Powell's speech and the release of the Fed's Beige Book. Important inflation data from the Eurozone and the US Core PCE Price Index are also due. Labor market indicators including JOLTS and ADP Nonfarm Employment change will provide further insights. The next Fed Interest Rate Decision is scheduled on December 10th, 2025.

30 November 2025

CNY - Manufacturing PMI (Nov)

Date: 30-Nov-25, Sun, 07:00 IST Currency: CNY Importance: 3 Forecast: 49.6 Previous: 49 Notes: The Chinese Manufacturing Purchasing Managers Index (PMI) provides an early indication each month of economic activities in the Chinese manufacturing sector. It is compiled by China Federation of Logistics & Purchasing (CFLP) and China Logistics Information Centre (CLIC). A higher than expected reading should be taken as positive/bullish for the CNY, while a lower than expected reading should be taken as negative/bearish for the CNY. Learn More: USDCNY
