Key Economic Events: Week Of 8th December 2025
Release Date: 8th December 2025
What to Expect in Markets This Week: This week is dominated by central bank decisions and inflation data. The Federal Reserve, Bank of Canada, Swiss National Bank, and Reserve Bank of Australia will announce their interest rate decisions. In the US, focus will be on JOLTS and Jobless Claims data. Additionally, Japan and the UK will release GDP figures, providing insights into global economic health. The next Fed Interest Rate Decision is scheduled on January 28th, 2026.
