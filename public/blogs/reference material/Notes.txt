Nvidia earnings are the main event next week, with investors keen for new insight into how the $4 trillion juggernaut at the heart of the artificial-intelligence boom is faring.
Quarterly reports from retailers Best Buy, Bath and Body Works and Urban Outfitters are also due.
Key economic data will include a July readout of the Federal Reserve's preferred inflation gauge, plus data on personal income and spending. Here's what to watch:
Monday, Aug. 25
Earnings: The aerospace company Heico (HEI) is due to report after markets close.

Fed speakers: New York Fed President John Williams.
Tuesday, Aug. 26
Economic data: Durable-goods orders for July are due at 8:30 a.m. ET. Also due are reports on housing prices and the Conference Board's consumer confidence survey.
Wednesday, Aug. 27
Nvidia earnings: Nvidia (NVDA) is due to report after the close.

Other results: Kohl’s (KSS) and Abercrombie & Fitch (ANF) are due before markets open, followed by Urban Outfitters (URBN), CrowdStrike (CRWD), Agilent Technologies (A) and HP (HPQ) after the close.

Tariff deadline: Indian exports to the U.S. face a hefty 50% tariff, a punishment for the country buying Russian oil, under an executive order President Trump signed earlier this month.
Thursday, Aug. 28
Earnings: Bath and Body Works (BBWI), Victoria’s Secret (VSCO) and Best Buy (BBY) are due to report before markets open. Ulta Beauty (ULTA), and Dell (DELL) are set to report after hours.

Economic data: Weekly jobless claims, plus a second estimate of April-June GDP. Trade swings and consumer spending helped power a 3% growth rate last quarter, a first estimate showed.

Fed speaker: Fed Governor Christopher Waller discusses the economic outlook.
Friday, Aug. 29
Earnings: The Chinese e-commerce giant Alibaba (BABA) is due to report.

Economic data: The July edition of the PCE price index, the Fed's preferred measure of inflation, is due at 8:30 a.m., alongside data on personal income and spending.
