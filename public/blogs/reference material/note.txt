The Federal Reserve meets next week and is expected to cut its key short-term interest rate for the first time in 2025. Investors are widely anticipating a 0.25 percentage point cut, but there is less agreement on what will follow at the central bank’s October and December meetings. Here’s what to watch for next week:
Monday, Sept. 15
Economic Data: The New York Fed’s Empire State manufacturing survey.
Tuesday, Sept. 16
Economic Data: Retail sales, export and import prices, industrial production and capacity utilization.

Federal Reserve Meeting: Day one of the Federal Reserve’s meeting on interest rates. Investors will be watching whether Trump adviser Stephen Miran will be confirmed by the Senate in time to attend the meeting as a Federal Reserve governor.

Data Talk: Former Bureau of Labor Statistics Commissioner Erika McEntarfer is due to give a lecture on the importance of data. It will be McEntarfer’s first public remarks since she was fired by President Trump in August.
Wednesday, Sept. 17
Fed Meeting: The Fed announces its decision on interest rates, and officials' economic projections, at 2 p.m. ET. Chair Jerome Powell discusses the decision and the outlook at a 2:30 p.m. press conference.

Other Central Banks: The Bank of Canada is due to make its own decision on interest rates. The country’s economy has been hit hard by tariffs in recent months.

Earnings: General Mills, Cracker Barrel

Economic Data: Housing starts data for August.

TikTok Deadline: The Chinese-owned app faces a U.S. ban, though the White House is likely to extend the deadline for the ban to go into effect. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is scheduled to meet with China Vice Premier He Lifeng during the week to discuss TikTok, as well as trade issues and money laundering.
Thursday, Sept. 18
Economic Data: Weekly jobless claims follow a high reading this past Thursday. Also, a report on leading economic indicators is due as well as the Philadelphia Fed manufacturing survey.

Central Banks: The Bank of England is expected to make its latest decision on interest rates.

Earnings: FedEx, Lennar, Darden Restaurants
Friday, Sept. 19
Central Banks: The Bank of Japan is due to make its latest decision on interest rates Friday in Tokyo, or late Thursday in the U.S.
